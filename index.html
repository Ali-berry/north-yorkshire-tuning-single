FAQ

Common questions

Do you come to me?

Yes. Whether you're at home, at work or somewhere else, we come to you and carry out the work on site, anywhere across North Yorkshire and Leeds.

How do I get a quote?

Call, text or WhatsApp us your registration and what you need. The price depends on your vehicle's make, model, engine and ECU type, so we quote each job individually.

Why is every job quoted individually?

Different vehicles need different tuning methods: OBD, bench or boot tuning. The method depends on your make, model, engine and ECU type.

How long does it take?

Most OBD remaps and coding jobs take one to two hours. Bench tuning takes longer because the ECU is removed.

Can a remap be put back to standard?

Yes. We save your vehicle's original software before we make any changes. If for any reason you're not happy with how it drives after the tune, we can put it back to the original factory settings.
